About this tool / Methods

Each row is one term of the PSI-MOD ontology (the PSI-MOD.obo release bundled with psimodpy, loaded with psimodpy.load(), obsolete terms included). Obsolete terms are hidden unless you change the Status filter.

  • Δ mass, mono: the monoisotopic mass difference of the modification (DiffMono in the OBO file, entry.diff_mono). Average: DiffAvg.
  • Δ formula: the difference formula (DiffFormula) in ProForma Hill notation (entry.proforma_formula).
  • Origin: the residue the modification occurs on; crosslinks list each site. Source: natural, artifact or hypothetical, as annotated in PSI-MOD.
